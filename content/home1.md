We believe many skilled investors lack the platform, infrastructure, and capital needed to scale. Riptide was created to provide that foundation and to give allocators earlier access to their performance.

## For Portfolio Managers

### Operating Solutions

Institutional infrastructure made easy.

Managers on Riptide operate with the systems and support typically reserved for large platforms. Everything you need to run a fund is set-up, from trading execution, portfolio risk analytics, and research systems to legal, financial ops, and fund administration – the Riptide platform is plug and play so you can focus on research and performance from day one.

Service providers:

- Execution and OMS: Eze
- Risk analytics: Arcana
- Portfolio management and reconciliation: Cartesian FinOps
- Trading: Riptide internal desk
- Prime brokerage: Goldman Sachs and Pershing
- Compliance: Salus GRC
- Research and data: Bloomberg, FactSet, InSync Analytics, and proprietary process enhancement and talent development systems.
- Legal, fund administration, and audit: Cole-Frieman, Ascent Group, and Citrin Cooperman

### Capital Solutions

A structured path for talent to grow.

Riptide supports portfolio managers at each stage of development through a clear progression:

### Riptide Opportunities Fund

Build an audited track record in an institutional environment with aligned capital.

### Riptide Flexible Equity Fund

Increase GMV pari passu with your current capital on a variable beta basis.

### Riptide Market Neutral Fund

Operate at scale within a multi-manager, high-Sharpe strategy while you build your portable track record with the aim of being seeded for a fund launch!

### Independent fund launch

Full operational support plus access to platform capital, SMAs, and external investors.

Managers retain ownership of their performance, while growing their long-term business value. Riptide provides coaching and support throughout the progression.

## For Capital Partners

### Strategic Solutions

Access to high-quality emerging managers.

Riptide provides strategic investors with institutional-grade diligence at the earliest stages of manager development. Investors gain visibility into the risk profile, return characteristics, and investment processes of emerging managers before they scale.

Our platform acts as an outsourced incubator. We support high-potential PMs with capital, infrastructure, and development resources, which allows strategic investors to:

- Access exclusive GP investment opportunities

- Participate in advantaged economics through revenue share or GP interests

- Build relationships with managers while their incentives and alignment are strongest

This structure helps allocators identify and partner with future high-performing managers at the point of greatest potential impact.

### Dedicated Managed Accounts

A flexible architecture designed for different investor needs.

Riptide offers a unified investing experience through dedicated managed accounts that can incorporate:

- Riptide Funds: Opportunities, Flexible Equity, and Market Neutral

- Partner funds and unique actively managed strategies

- Tax-aware strategies designed to offset capital gains and ordinary income

- Self-directed trading with dedicated resources

All investments can sit within one account to maintain tax efficiency, simplify reporting, and provide complete transparency into holdings and performance.
