# How We Serve

## Investment Managers

We offer emerging and established investment talent a supportive platform to build verifiable, portable track records, backed by robust business formation support and access to diversified capital. Our scalable infrastructure features AI-driven research and integrated risk management tools, enabling managers to focus on their investment process. We seek individuals who value a balance of autonomy and partnership that is elusive in more traditional alternatives, while aligning success across all Riptide stakeholders. Rather than impose a rigid one-size-fits-all framework, we empower our managers with the resources and flexibility needed to maximize their potential.

## Capital Partners

We create alignment in our strategies through direct management investment. We match this alignment with high-performance coaching and cutting-edge systems to optimize risk-adjusted returns. Our strategic partners gain access to talent and all our LPs invest alongside motivated managers at peak performance. We design our investment products to align the right talent with the right capital partner objectives to maximize outcomes for managers, capital partners and the firm.
