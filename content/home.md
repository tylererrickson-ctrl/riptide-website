## RIPTIDE ADVISORS

Riptide Advisors is a multi-manager hedge fund platform that allows emerging portfolio managers to launch strategies and established managers to operate independent strategies with dedicated support, trading, middle, and back-office, compliance, and risk monitoring systems. We offer investors two multi-manager funds and custom managed accounts to access the alpha of our strategies.

# FOR CAPITAL PARTNERS

We provide capital partners with differentiated access to high-quality investment strategies, supported by institutional-grade infrastructure and disciplined risk management.

#### MULTI-MANAGER FUNDS

##### A flexible architecture designed for different investor needs.

Riptide offers two multi-manager funds designed to generate uncorrelated, alpha-rich returns. Capital is deployed via SMAs allowing investments to sit within one fund to maximize risk management, maintain tax efficiency, simplify reporting, and provide complete transparency into holdings and performance.

[EXPLORE MORE](/for-investors){.button}

#### CUSTOM MANAGED ACCOUNTS

##### Access to high-quality managers and special situations.

Investors gain visibility into the risk profile, return characteristics, and investment processes of emerging managers before they scale. Our platform acts as an outsourced incubator where allocators can invest directly into managers when the time is right. The platform also offers direct investment into special situations originated by our managers.
