# Principles

Guiding Values for Our Firm

We earn trust from our partners by creating undeniable alignment—building lasting partnerships that empower us to play to win today and improve constantly for tomorrow.

## Earn Trust

“The highest form a civilization can reach is a seamless web on deserved trust.” – Charlie Munger

We believe trust is the foundation of long-term success. We are committed to treating every individual—partners, team members, and investors—with respect and honesty. Our decisions and actions are guided by a dedication to ethical conduct, open communication, and transparency. We synthesize the objectives of managers, capital partners and the firm to reach optimal outcomes for all parties.

Importantly, trust is earned over time through consistent communication, transparency and results. We preach patience to our managers and align their success with capital partners who invest over the long term.

## Create Alignment

“He whose life has a why can bear almost any how.” – Nietzsche

Our management team invests in the business, and all our managers invest in their own strategies. We put our capital at risk because we believe in undeniable alignment with our capital partners. We have created investment mandates that match a diverse set of talent so managers can generate value through strategies that match the goals of our capital partner.

Our funds align incentives to maximize outcomes. As managers perform, our capital partners invest along the way. We believe managers and capital partners will reinvest in the system as we succeed, creating a positive cycle of future opportunities.

## Build Lasting Partnerships

“If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together.” – African Proverb

Enduring success is built on strong, mutually beneficial relationships. By fostering open dialogue and consistently delivering on our commitments, we aim to cultivate alliances that stand the test of time.

Long-term partnerships allow us to:

• Coordinate efficiently and spend less time verifying and more time executing.

• Grow exponentially through reputation, referrals, and mutual success.

Importantly, we want partners that believe in our long-term value, because:

• In hard times: partners lean in and we emerge stronger together.

• In good times: partners lean in and amplify our success.

Lasting partnerships build durable systems of reciprocity, loyalty, and trust offering stability for all stakeholders.

## Play to Win

“The credit belongs to the man who is actually in the arena.” – Theodore Roosevelt

Our team approaches every challenge with determination, ambition, and a focus on excellence. "Play to win" means setting clear goals and holding ourselves accountable for results. We embrace calculated risks, think creatively, and pursue competitive advantages that drive superior outcomes for our investors and our firm. While we compete vigorously, we do so with integrity and respect for the rules of the game.

Playing to win is a posture of radical responsibility, highlighted by our belief that all managers should invest personally alongside the firm’s capital partners.

Our managers enter the arena, own their decisions, absorb consequences, and learn from their mistakes. Importantly, winning is not the only reward, becoming someone capable of winning over the long-term is the true objective.

## Improve Constantly

“If you want to get somewhere else, you must run at least twice as fast as that.” – Lewis Carroll

We are committed to innovating, adapting, and growing in all aspects of our business. By regularly evaluating our performance, seeking feedback, and investing in our talent, we ensure that our strategies, processes, and services remain cutting-edge and effective.

In competitive markets, the forces around you are constantly improving. Standing still is equivalent to falling behind.

We move with speed and precision to achieve small gains every day that compound into large competitive advantages over time.
