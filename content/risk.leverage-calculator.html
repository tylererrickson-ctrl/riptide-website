Model caveats

  • Parametric VaR assumes i.i.d. returns and √t scaling — realized tails, vol clustering, and gap risk can be materially worse. The Student-t toggle is a partial correction.
  • Loss tolerance is equity loss at inception leverage. Effective leverage rises as equity erodes mid-drawdown; the model assumes no re-levering spiral and no forced deleveraging.
  • "Any point in window" mode uses the reflection principle (breach probability ≈ 2× endpoint probability for a driftless walk) — this is the honest constraint if margin triggers bind intraweek.
  • A breach probability per window compounds across repeated windows — over a year of independent windows the chance of at least one breach is far higher.
  • Ignores financing costs, margin haircut changes, and volatility estimation error entering regime shifts.
